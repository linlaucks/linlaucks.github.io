TRANSPORT

ARRIVAL

The hotel block and Ceremony Site are located between O'Hare and Midway airports.

By public transport, it is roughly 1.25 hours between either airport and the Ceremony Site. If you are renting a car or taking an Uber, it is roughly 30 minutes from O'Hare and 20 minutes from Midway.

CITY ACCESS

From the hotel block, the city-center is roughly 40 minutes away by public transit, and 25 minutes away by car.

CEREMONY TRANSPORT

There will be no transport provided from the hotel block to the ceremony, but there will be a bus/trolley to take guests back to the hotel block after the reception.
